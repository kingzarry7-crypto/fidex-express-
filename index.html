GPS Proof of Delivery adds another layer of visibility by showing the nearest coordinates to where your package was marked as delivered. This feature, which is available in your shipper account, helps you quickly confirm the delivery location with map-based accuracy. The delivery location is displayed on a map, providing a clear visual reference of where the package was left.
